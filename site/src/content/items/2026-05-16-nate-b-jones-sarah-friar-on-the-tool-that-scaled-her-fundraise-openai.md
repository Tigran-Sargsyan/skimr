## Notes

## Context of the Discussion
The speaker is reflecting on a recent fundraising effort and how an AI tool factored into it. They specifically mention having talked “quite often” about how helpful this tool was in the latest fundraise, suggesting it was a recurring topic and a notable part of their experience.

## Role of the AI Tool in Fundraising
The tool was used to help “keep track of everything” during the fundraising process. This implies it supported organizational and coordination tasks rather than performing sophisticated technical analysis. Despite this seemingly simple use case, the speaker associates the tool with a very large outcome: they describe the effort as “the largest private fund raise in the history,” and say the AI tool was “instrumental” in that process.

## Non-Technical but High-Impact Use Case
The speaker highlights a contrast between the scale of the result and the simplicity of how the tool was used. They describe this as a “very non-technical use” for the AI system. This indicates that even basic, administrative or logistical applications of AI can have major leverage in complex, high-stakes activities like large fundraising rounds.

## Breadth of Potential Applications
From this example, the speaker generalizes to the wider potential of the tool. They state that “the amount of use cases” is vast, shifting the central question to “how creative are you?” This frames the system as broadly capable across many domains, with user imagination being the main driver of new applications.

## Capability and Limits
The speaker is careful to note that the tool is “not capable of everything,” acknowledging real limits. However, they immediately add that it is capable of “almost everything,” emphasizing its wide applicability. Together, these remarks underline a view of AI tools as powerful general-purpose assistants whose practical value depends heavily on how people choose to deploy them, even in seemingly mundane, non-technical tasks.
