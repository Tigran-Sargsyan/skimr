## Key Points

- Total users recently reached 4 million and are on track to hit 5 million soon.
- User growth is accelerating rather than slowing as the base gets larger.
- The share of users doing general-purpose, non-coding work is increasing over time.
- Non-coding usage is growing faster than coding-related usage among the user base.
- Week-over-week growth for non-coding usage was about 40% in a recent comparison.
- This rapid non-coding growth suggests it will overtake the coding-oriented product, Codex, soon.

## Notes

## Overall User Growth
- The product has recently reached 4 million users, marking a significant milestone.
- Growth is described as “phenomenal” and “accelerating,” implying that the rate of new user acquisition is increasing rather than tapering off.
- Based on the current trajectory, they expect to hit 5 million users soon, indicating rapid scaling within a short timeframe between milestones.

## Shift Toward General-Purpose, Non-Coding Use
- A notable trend is that a growing fraction of users are using the product for general-purpose, non-coding work rather than for coding.
- This non-coding, general-purpose segment is expanding faster than the overall user base, which itself is already growing quickly.
- As a result, the relative share of coders within the user base is shrinking compared with non-coding users.

## Week-Over-Week Non-Coding Surge
- Comparing last week to this week, non-coding usage experienced approximately 40% week-over-week growth.
- This 40% increase is framed as especially remarkable because it is higher than the overall growth rate and higher than growth among coding-focused users.

## Implications for Codex
- Given this trajectory, there is an expectation that general-purpose, non-coding usage will “take over Codex very, very quickly.”
- This suggests a future where the primary use of the system shifts from being coding-centric (as with Codex) to being dominated by broader, non-programming applications.
- The conversation frames this shift as both rapid and somewhat unexpected, emphasizing that the non-coding segment is the main driver of recent growth.
